Biography

I am a fashion model and actor with a strong passion for the fashion and entertainment industry.

I began my modelling journey at the age of 16 and was honoured with the title of Star Miss Teen Punjab 2022, later representing Punjab in Star Miss Teen India 2022. With experience in theatre, dance, modelling, and brand collaborations including Renne Cosmetics, Peddlers, and Boss Rides, I continue to grow as a versatile performer. Recently crowned Miss Akimbo Models India 2026, I aspire to establish a strong presence in modelling, acting, and media while continuously evolving as an artist.

My mission is to represent with elegance through fashion and modeling.
